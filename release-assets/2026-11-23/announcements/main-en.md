# What’s new in GF — schedules, swaps and shift corrections

- Schedules are grouped by month. Tap View More to preview other published schedules you can access.
- Shift corrections now guide you through each step. Request different hours, a shift on a day off, or a day off in place of a scheduled shift.
- Where the rules allow it, offer separate, non-overlapping parts of the same shift.
- The schedule shows badges for pending corrections, open swaps and completed swaps.
- Open App Guide from Profile for step-by-step instructions and screenshots. You can choose the guide’s language separately.

Open Schedules to explore the updates, or use the guide to follow each step at your own pace.

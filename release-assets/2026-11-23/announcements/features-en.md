## Choose a schedule by month

Tap the schedule name in Shifts to see your schedules grouped by month. Choose one to open it, or tap the eye icon to switch from the monthly table to a view of the chosen day.

**Attachment** `01-schedule-selection.gif` or `02-schedule-views.gif`

## Request a shift correction or a day off

In Shifts, tap Adjust and choose the schedule and shift. Change the hours in 15-minute steps or request a day off in place of the scheduled shift. Check the review screen and send your request for manager approval.

**Attachment** `03-shift-correction.gif` or `04-request-day-off.gif`

## Offer part of a shift

In Swap, offer the full shift or choose specific hours where the rules allow it. If you offer 09:00–15:00 from a 09:00–21:00 shift, 15:00–21:00 may still be available for a separate offer.

**Attachment** `05-partial-shift-swap.gif`

## Check your swap status in the schedule

A blue icon means your offer is waiting for acceptance. A green icon means the transfer or exchange is complete. Tap the icon in the matrix or daily view to read what the status means.

**Attachment** `06-swap-status.gif`

## Where to check your work hours

In Shifts, scroll below the schedule to Work hours. Choose the month and year to see the dates, scheduled hours across your schedules, and the total for that month.

**Attachment** `07-work-hours.gif`

## Explore the GF App Guide

Open App Guide from Profile and choose the language you want to read it in. The illustrated steps cover installing GF, finding a schedule, requesting a correction and offering a swap. Your language on the other GF screens stays the same.

**Attachment** `08-app-guide.gif`
